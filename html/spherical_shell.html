Convention

Force units are GMm/R². Positive means inward (−x); negative means outward (+x). At the center these signs refer to the chosen x-axis. The shell has mass M, test mass m, and radius R.

What the two lower plots mean

Both use the original ring angle θ to label the same mass dm. The right graph is a bookkeeping decomposition of a point mass into the original ring masses, all now at the center. It is not a second spatial ring.

dF_shell = Gm dm cosα/s²; dF_center = Gm dm/r²

On the right, Gm/r² is constant: adding all dm gives M immediately. On the left, the distance and projection vary; the derivation below shows why their weighted sum gives the same result outside.

F_center = GMm/r² × ∫₀π ½sinθ dθ = GMm/r²

1. Add rings, not individual points

Measure θ from the axis toward the test mass. A ring has circumference 2πR sin θ and width R dθ. Opposite azimuths cancel transverse forces.

σ = M/(4πR²), dm = 2πσR² sinθ dθ

2. Project the attraction onto the inward axis

Let s be the distance from the ring to m, and α the angle between its pull and the inward axis. The axial component can be negative when the test mass lies inside the shell.

s² = r² + R² − 2rR cosθ
cosα = (r − R cosθ)/s = (r² + s² − R²)/(2rs)
dFᵢₙ = Gm dm cosα/s²

3. Replace θ with distance s

2s ds = 2rR sinθ dθ
dFᵢₙ = GMm/(4Rr²) [1 + (r² − R²)/s²] ds

For r > 0 and r ≠ R, the nearest distance is |r − R| and the farthest is r + R.

Fᵢₙ = GMm/(4Rr²) [s − (r² − R²)/s] from |r − R| to r + R

4. Evaluate the two endpoints

Outside (r > R), the bracket is 2R − (−2R) = 4R. Inside (0 < r < R), it is 2R − 2R = 0.

F = 0 inside; F = GMm/r² outside.

At r = 0, symmetry gives zero directly. At r = R, the ideal surface field is discontinuous; this visualization leaves its value unset and shows both one-sided limits.

A spherically symmetric planet is a stack of shells. Outside the entire planet, their forces add to GMm/r². Inside a solid planet, only the mass enclosed within r contributes.

Each circular ring pulls along its radial lines. Components perpendicular to the center-to-test axis cancel in pairs, leaving only the inward axial component.
