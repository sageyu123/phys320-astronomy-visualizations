What is a flyby?
A planetesimal on an orbit that crosses a planet’s orbit sometimes passes close to the planet. Seen from the planet, it swings around on a hyperbola and leaves in a new direction with the same speed: a gravitational slingshot. Seen from the Sun, its speed has changed, so its orbit has changed: a larger or smaller orbit. Energy and angular momentum are conserved, so the planet’s orbit changes the opposite way, by an amount smaller by the mass ratio. Thousands of such flybys (Migration tab) move the planets.
