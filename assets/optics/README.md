# Detector illustration sources

The five original PNG images below were supplied by the user on September 17, 2026. They replace the previously downloaded NASA images. No photographer or mission attribution was supplied; do not carry over the earlier images' credits.

- `moon-provided.png`: Moon image.
- `sun-provided.png`: Sun image.
- `jupiter-provided.png`: Jupiter image.
- `saturn-provided.png`: Saturn with rings. The illustrative 40-arcsecond preset refers to the outer ring span, not the planet's disk.
- `venus-provided.png`: Venus. The 20-arcsecond preset is an illustrative, editable angular diameter.

The images retain their original aspect ratios and are cropped at draw time. Displayed disk diameter (outer-ring width for Saturn) follows the geometric image-size calculation. Photographic detail is illustrative, not a simulation of telescope resolution, diffraction, seeing, or detector sampling.
